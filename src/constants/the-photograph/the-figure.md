**Maya couldn’t stop looking at the photograph.**

Maya didn’t go to work that morning. She sat on the edge of her bed and looked at the photograph. She had taken it herself. She was sure of it. 

The room in the photograph was exactly the same as her room. The chair was beside the window. The lamp was on. The curtains were half open. But she was asleep in the bed. Maya looked at the clock. 

**_7:03AM._**

She looked back at the photograph. _“How?”_ She turned it over. 

Nothing.

She checked the camera. The new roll was still inside. She counted the photographs. 

**_One had been used._**

She remembered taking the photograph of her room the night before. She did not remember taking another one.

...

Maya picked up her phone. She called Priya. _“Can you come over?”_

_“What happened?”_ Priya asked.

_“I need you to look at something.”_

Priya arrived twenty minutes later. Maya handed her the photograph. Priya looked at it. Then she looked at Maya. _“Is that you?”_

_“Yes.”_

_“When did you take this?”_

_“I did’nt.”_

Priya looked at the photograph again. _“Maybe you took it before you went to sleep.”_

_“I was awake when I took the last picture.”_ Maya said.

_“Then may be you took another one later.”_

Maya frowned. _“I don’t remember.”_

Priya was quiet for a moment.

Maya pointed at the camera. _“The new roll was inside. One picture was used.”_

Priya looked at the camera. _“Then take another one tonight.”_ Maya looked at her, confused. _“Why?”_

_“To see if it happens again.”_

Maya nodded slowly. She wasn’t sure she wanted to do it. But she wanted to know what was happening.

...

That night, Maya placed the camera on her bedside table. She checked the room one more time. The chair was beside the window. The curtains were half open. The lamp was off. 

Everything was normal. Maya looked at the camera for a moment. Then she picked it up. 

**Click!**

The photograph came out. Maya placed it beside the other photograph. She looked at it for a moment. Nothing seemed unusual. She switched off the light and went to bed.

...

The next morning, Maya woke up early. The first thing she saw was the camera. Then she noticed the photograph beside it. She picked it up. 

At first, she smiled. 

Everything looked exactly the same. 
Her bed. 
The lamp. 
The curtains. 
The chair. 

Then she noticed the bedroom door. Maya slowly looked towards it. Her door was closed. She looked back at the photograph.

The door was open.

Maya stared at it.

And someone was standing in the doorway.
