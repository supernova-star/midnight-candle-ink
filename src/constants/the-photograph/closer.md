**The room felt different that night.**

Maya couldn’t take her eyes off the photograph. Someone was standing in the doorway. She looked at the real door.

It was closed.

She looked back at the photograph.

The figure was still there.

Maya felt a chill run through her. She picked up her phone and called Priya.

_“Are you awake?”_

Priya sounded sleepy. _“What happened?”_

_“I need you to come over.”_

...

Twenty minutes later, Priya arrived.

Maya handed her the photograph without saying anything. Priya looked at it. Her expression changed. _“Who is that?”_

_“I don’t know.”_ Maya replied quietly.

_“Can you see the face?”_

Maya shook her head. _“No.”_

Priya looked at the photograph again. _“Was your door open last night?”_

_“No. I always lock it.”_

Priya looked toward the bedroom. Then back at Maya. _“Hey, don’t worry. We’ll figure this out.”_ She paused and looked at the photograph again.

_“Let’s record tonight. If something is happening while you’re asleep, we’ll see it.”_

Maya nodded.

...

That night, Maya placed her phone on a shelf facing the bed. She started recording.

Priya checked the door.

Locked.

She checked the windows.

Closed.

Everything was normal.

Maya placed the Polaroid camera on her bedside table. She looked at it for a moment. Then she turned off the light and went to bed.

...

The next morning, Maya woke up before her alarm. The first thing she saw was her phone. She picked it up and checked the recording.

She watched herself get into bed. She watched herself fall asleep. Nothing happened. The door stayed closed. Nobody entered the room.

Maya watched the recording again. Still nothing.

She let out a slow breath.

Then she noticed something beside the Polaroid camera.

**_A photograph._**

Maya froze.

She hadn’t taken one. Neither had Priya. She picked it up. At first, everything looked normal.

Her bed.  
The lamp.  
The curtains.  
The chair.

Then she noticed the figure. It was no longer standing in the doorway. It had moved. It was standing just inside the room.

Maya looked at the real room. The door was closed. She looked back at the photograph. The figure was closer to the bed.

_“Priya.”_ Maya screamed.

Priya ran into Maya’s room. She looked at the photograph. Her face changed.

_“It moved.”_

Maya nodded.

Neither of them spoke.

...

Priya picked up Maya’s phone. _“Let’s check the recording again.”_

They watched it from the beginning. Maya got into bed. She turned off the light. The room stayed quiet. Maya slept. The door stayed closed. Nothing moved. The recording ended. Priya looked at Maya. _“There’s nothing here.”_

Maya nodded slowly. _“Then how did the photograph change?”_

Neither of them had an answer. 

Priya looked at the camera. _“Maybe it’s the camera.”_

Maya frowned. _“What do you mean?”_

_“What if the camera is taking the photographs by itself?”_

Maya stared at it.

Priya picked up the camera and pointed towards the closed bedroom door.

**Click.**

The photograph came out. They waited. Priya picked it up. The door was closed. Nothing unusual.

Maya let out a breath.

Then Priya’s expression changed.

_“Maya…”_

Maya looked at her. Priya slowly turned the photograph toward her.

The figure was standing beside the bed.
