**Maya needed a gift!**

Her best friend, Priya, was turning thirty that weekend, and Maya wanted to give her something different. She had already looked at perfumes, books, bags and jewellery. Nothing felt right.

On Saturday afternoon, Maya walked into a small second-hand shop near her office. The shop was narrow and dusty. There were old books on one side and cameras on the other. Priya liked old things. Maya walked around slowly, looking at things she didn’t really need.

Then she saw it. A small Polaroid camera! It was old and cream-colored. There were a few scratches on the sides. Maya picked it up.

*“Does this still work?” she asked.*

The shopkeeper looked at the camera.

*“Yes. There are four pictures left inside.”*

Maya smiled. *“Can I get a new roll for it too?”*

The shopkeeper reached under the counter and took out a small box of instant film. *“This should work.”*

Maya paid for both. She thought it would make a cute gift for Priya.

...

That evening, Maya took the camera home. She wanted to try it before giving it away. There were four photographs left inside.

She took a picture of her coffee mug. Click!

The photograph came out. She waited. The image slowly appeared. Her coffee mug looked nice and warm in the photograph. She smiled.

She took another picture of the plant near her window. Click!

She waited for the photograph to develop. The plant looked pretty in the soft evening light. Maya smiled and placed it beside the first photograph. There were two pictures left. This time she turned the camera toward herself.

*“Let’s see how this works,” she said.*

She held the camera at arm’s length.

**Click!**

The photograph came out. Maya waited. Her face slowly appeared. The picture was slightly blurry. Her hair was messy and she was smiling at the camera. She laughed. *“Not bad.”* She placed it beside the other two. She looked around the room.

It was almost midnight. The warm light from the lamp fell across the room. The curtains moved slightly with the breeze, and the last bit of evening light came through the window. Her room looked unusually beautiful.

She picked up the camera. She wanted to keep a picture of it.

**Click!**

The photograph came out. Maya placed it on her desk and waited.

Her bedroom slowly appeared. Her bed. Her lamp. Her curtains. Her wooden chair near the window. Everything looked so warm and cozy. She smiled. She liked this one the most.

She left all photographs neatly arranged on her bed side table. Then she opened the new roll she bought from the shop. She put it into the camera.
The camera was ready for Priya now. Maya switched off the light and went to bed.

...

The next morning, Maya woke up to the sound of her alarm. She reached for her phone. Then she noticed the photograph on her side table.

She picked it up.

At first everything looked normal. Her bed. The lamp. The curtains. The chair.

Then she looked closer. Her heart skipped!

**_“No! Thats not possible!”_**
