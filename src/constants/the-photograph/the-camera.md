# The Meeting

Maya had never liked old photographs. **There was something unsettling** about the way people stared back from them.  
But when she found the photograph in the attic, she couldn't look away.

> "Some photographs are better left forgotten."

She turned it over and found _her own name_ written on the back.

---

## The Photograph

The date was **September 18, 1998** — three years before Maya was born.

---

The room suddenly felt colder.

And that was when she heard it.

_A faint whisper, like someone calling her name._

---

"Maya."

She froze.
